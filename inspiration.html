What shapes my work.

A running map of where I start, what I return to, and how I turn references and research into something useful.

STEP 01 — MY VALUES

Clear. Useful. Intentional. Human.

These are the words I keep running through my head while I design. They’ve become a quiet signature in the way I approach almost everything I make. They keep the standard high while still leaving room to learn, adapt, and figure things out along the way.
